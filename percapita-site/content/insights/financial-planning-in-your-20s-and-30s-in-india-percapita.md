**Money Moves in Your 20s & 30s: What Actually Matters?**

Your 20s and 30s can feel like one giant financial group project.

Salary. Rent. EMIs. Travel. Family. Investments. Maybe a business.

And somehow, everyone expects you to have a five-year plan.

Relax.

You don’t need to have everything figured out.

But a few financial basics can make a difference.

**Start with a safety net**

Before thinking only about investments, consider having accessible savings for unexpected expenses.

Your emergency fund is not trying to beat the market.

**It’s there for the plot twists.**

**Don’t ignore insurance**

As your responsibilities grow, your protection needs can change.

Health and life insurance may play an important role depending on your circumstances.

**Invest with a goal**

Instead of asking:

**“What investment is giving the highest return?”**

Start with:

**“What am I investing for?”**

Then consider your timeline, risk profile and liquidity needs.

**Keep debt in check**

Not all debt works the same way.

Understand your interest rate, repayment schedule, total cost and how the loan affects your monthly cash flow.

**Let your plan evolve**

Your first salary won’t be your last.

Your priorities will change. Your income may change. Your responsibilities will change.

Your financial approach can evolve too.

**The Percapita takeaway**

You don’t need a perfect financial life.

You need to start understanding it.

**Small decisions. Clearer thinking. Better context.**

That’s a pretty good place to start.

*Investment decisions involve risk. Consider your financial situation, objectives and risk profile before investing. Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully.*
