**PMS vs Mutual Funds: What Actually Changes?**

PMS and mutual funds both involve professional investment management.

But they’re definitely not the same thing.

Let’s break it down without the finance dictionary.

**Mutual Funds**

With a mutual fund, money from multiple investors is pooled into a scheme and managed according to its stated investment objective.

You own units of the mutual fund scheme.

**PMS**

Portfolio Management Services offer a more customised portfolio-management structure.

Under PMS, investments are managed according to the agreed strategy and mandate, with securities held in the investor’s name subject to the applicable structure.

**What’s different?**

Some key differences include:

* **Structure:** Pooled scheme vs individually managed portfolio
* **Customisation:** PMS can provide a more tailored portfolio approach
* **Minimum investment:** PMS has specific regulatory minimums
* **Costs:** Fee structures can differ
* **Risk:** Both involve market risk, but strategies and portfolios can vary significantly

**So, which one is better?**

That’s the wrong question.

The better question is:

**Which structure makes sense for your situation?**

Your portfolio size, objectives, risk profile, investment horizon, liquidity needs and experience all matter.

**The Percapita takeaway**

Don’t choose based on the label.

Understand **how the product works, what it costs, what the risks are, and where it fits in your overall portfolio.**

*Investments are subject to market risks. Read all relevant documents carefully before investing.*
