**Your First Investment Checklist: Before You Hit Invest**

Your first investment can feel like a big deal.

And honestly, it should.

Not because investing needs to be scary — but because putting your money somewhere deserves a little thought before you hit **“Invest.”**

Here’s your starter checklist.

**01 — Know your why**

What are you investing for?

Emergency fund? Education? A home? Retirement? Long-term wealth creation?

Your goal influences the kind of investment you may consider.

**02 — Know your timeline**

When will you need the money?

A goal that’s a few months away is very different from one that’s decades away.

**03 — Know your risk**

Ask yourself how much volatility you can realistically handle — financially and emotionally.

Risk isn’t just a number on a form.

**04 — Check your liquidity**

Could you need this money unexpectedly?

Before locking money into an investment, understand when and how you can access it.

**05 — Understand what you’re buying**

Don’t invest because:

“Someone on Instagram said…”

Read the product information. Understand the strategy, costs, risks and conditions.

**06 — Look at the bigger picture**

One investment doesn’t equal a financial plan.

Consider your existing investments, insurance, liabilities, cash flow and goals.

**The Percapita takeaway**

You don’t need to know everything before you start.

But you should know **what you’re investing in, why you’re investing, and what risks you’re taking.**
