**AIFs Explained: What Are Alternative Investments?**

Alternative Investment Funds sound complicated.

The basic idea is actually pretty straightforward:

**They invest through strategies that sit outside the traditional mutual-fund route.**

**What is an AIF?**

An Alternative Investment Fund is a privately pooled investment vehicle established under SEBI’s regulatory framework and operates according to its specific category, strategy and offering documents.

AIFs are broadly classified into **Category I, Category II and Category III**, with different investment strategies and regulatory characteristics.

**Why consider alternatives?**

Alternative strategies can provide exposure to areas or approaches that may not be available through traditional investment products in the same way.

But they can also come with different:

* Risk profiles
* Liquidity conditions
* Fee structures
* Investment horizons
* Eligibility requirements

**What should you understand first?**

Before considering an AIF, look beyond the name.

Understand:

**Strategy → Structure → Risk → Liquidity → Fees → Eligibility**

That’s the real checklist.

**AIF vs mutual funds**

They serve different purposes and operate under different structures.

Neither should be viewed in isolation.

The right question is whether an alternative investment fits within your **overall financial and portfolio context.**

**The Percapita takeaway**

Alternative doesn’t mean automatically better.

It means **different**.

And different deserves a closer look.

*Alternative Investment Funds involve risk and are subject to applicable regulatory, eligibility, liquidity and investment requirements. Read all relevant documents carefully before investing.*
